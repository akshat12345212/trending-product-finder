# Methodology

The workflow separates candidate discovery, evidence collection, qualification and review. It emits every qualifying product in the supplied candidate universe, both as an international list and an India opportunity list. The six pilot candidates are explicit search hypotheses, not a claim that they are currently viral. Extend `candidates.json` to widen the search; no final shortlist limit exists. Public mode follows page-provided month/Shorts filters, reads the first YouTube result page and the returned videos' embedded metadata. It is a limited fallback. The Apify path searches TikTok and YouTube Shorts, retains run provenance and retrieves all dataset pages within a declared acquisition budget.

“Currently trending” means published within 30 days and measured within seven days. A qualifying video needs at least 10,000 views and `(likes + comments) / views ≥ 4%`. This is a declared screening threshold, not a claimed industry benchmark: the view floor reduces tiny-sample noise, while the engagement floor prioritizes response over reach. Missing or rounded metrics remain unknown. A single observation measures recent high engagement, not growth velocity or unique engaged people. A production follow-up should compare repeated snapshots and test threshold sensitivity.

A product requires at least 50 distinct qualifying short videos, two platforms contributing at least five each, five evidenced countries contributing at least three each, and ten platform-specific creator identities. At most five videos per creator contribute. Canonical video identity removes tracking and URL aliases. Caption/title phrase matching is conservative but imperfect; ambiguous matches require review. Cross-platform reposts and common ownership may remain undetected. Country evidence means a documented upload location or verified creator location, never search locale, language, proxy location, or a list of countries where a video is playable. It does not establish audience geography.

Shipping eligibility requires sourced confirmation of a packed weight no greater than 1 kg, longest packed side no greater than 40 cm, non-fragile construction and no applicable restriction for the intended shipping routes. These are operational assumptions, not universal carrier or legal rules. Unverified products stay pending. Batteries, liquids, magnets, regulated products and intellectual-property concerns need specific review. No shipping clearance has been inferred from a product name.

The India mode applies the same trend and shipping gates, then requires fewer than five verified active sellers. Discovery covers Amazon.in, Flipkart, Instagram and Shopify stores, including aliases. Search hits are only leads: a reviewer must verify the exact product, seller identity, stock and delivery to India. Multiple listings and cross-channel storefronts sharing one business are merged by `seller_id`. All four channel checks must be documented within seven days. Incomplete coverage yields an unknown estimate, not zero. Even a completed search produces an estimate within its documented scope, not proof of market-wide absence. A clear customer problem, demonstrable benefit and differentiation hypothesis are required to frame an early brand opportunity; low competition alone is insufficient.

The supplied public run preserves real observations and collection failures. Missing second-platform access, geography, seller verification or shipping evidence cannot be repaired with invented values. The supplier-cost bonus is omitted because no comparable delivered quotes were obtained. Paid API execution requires separately configured provider access; its integration tests use labelled synthetic responses, never live output.
